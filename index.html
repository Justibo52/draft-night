What the numbers mean

GP Games played this season/view.
PTS Goals + assists (skaters only).
FPTS Total fantasy points, using the current Scoring Weights.
VALUE FPTS-per-game, shrunk so a hot short stretch doesn't outrank a great full season, nudged ±15% by how open that position still is on your My Team roster (Roster Needs), and nudged up to another ±10% by how thin the whole remaining pool is at that position (skaters only) — a great player at a scarce position (e.g. the last strong center) gets a small boost over an equally-good player at a deep one. All soft tilts, never a filter. This is the default sort — see HOW_TO_USE.md for the full explanation. Every number driving this (the shrinkage strength, both swing amounts, the assumed league size) is editable in the Scoring Weights panel's Ranking Logic dropdown.
Proj % Where preseason projections rank this player league-wide (100 = best).
Spread How much projection sources disagree — low = confident, high = risk/injury/role uncertainty.
Δ Proj % minus this player's historical percentile — green/up = trending better than their track record, red/down = trending worse.
